# Glossary

**Aerospike**: a nozzle type with a much wider expansion-ratio range (2 to 500) than the bell nozzle (2 to 100). See [Engine Design Reference](Engine-Design-Reference.md#exhaust-packs-nozzles).

**Arrival height**: the Y level at which rockets and players appear when they travel to a world.

**Basin**: Create's block that holds fluids and items for the Mechanical Mixer-style machines. The Mechanical Electrolyzer and Catalyst Carrier work above one.

**Blisk**: "bladed disk": the turbine wheel part of an engine turbine.

**Blueprint (Engine)**: the item that stores one engine design and starts every assembly recipe. See [Blueprints and Designs](Blueprints-and-Designs.md).

**Blueprint (Design)**: the paper that teaches you a power pack or exhaust pack.

**Chamber pressure**: the pressure inside the engine's combustion chamber, in bar. Must stay below what the engine's material can hold.

**Combustion efficiency**: a power pack's number (0 to 1) that scales how hot and how efficient the burn is.

**Contraption**: a structure of blocks that Create turns into a moving entity. A rocket is one.

**Cryogenic**: very cold. All the mod's fluids are cryogenic liquids.

**Δv (delta-v)**: the total change in speed a rocket can deliver, in m/s. Trips between worlds have a Δv cost. See [Rocket Science Reference](Rocket-Science-Reference.md).

**Dry mass**: the rocket's mass without its propellant.

**Expansion ratio (ε)**: the ratio of the nozzle's exit area to its narrowest point (throat). Bigger gives more ISP and a heavier engine.

**Exhaust pack**: the nozzle sub-assembly of an engine (Bell Nozzle or Aerospike).

**Heated / Superheated**: Create's basin heat levels, from a lit Blaze Burner and a Blaze-Cake-fuelled Blaze Burner.

**ISP (specific impulse)**: engine efficiency, in seconds. Higher is better; a 300 s engine gets more push from each kilogram of fuel than a 200 s one.

**LH₂, LOX, LCH₄**: liquid hydrogen, liquid oxygen, liquid methane.

**mB (millibucket)**: one thousandth of a bucket. 1 mB is 1 litre.

**Mixture ratio**: the proportion in which an engine consumes its propellants.

**Monopropellant**: a single fluid that burns on its own (the mod's "Premixed methalox").

**Orbit (world)**: Earth Orbit, Moon Orbit or Mars Orbit: zero-gravity void worlds.

**Power pack**: the pump-and-burner sub-assembly of an engine (Open Cycle, Full Flow, …).

**Propellant**: the fluid or fluids an engine burns.

**Regolith**: loose, dusty rock on the Moon and Mars. Washing it can yield metal nuggets.

**RPM / SU**: Create's rotation speed and stress units.

**Sealer**: short for the Oxygen Sealer.

**Sequenced assembly**: Create's multi-step crafting with deployers, presses and other machines on a belt. Engine parts use it.

**Spacesuit**: helmet + oxygen backtank + leggings + boots.

**Thrust**: the force an engine pushes with, in newtons.

**Thrust-to-weight ratio**: thrust ÷ weight. Must be at least 1 to lift off.

**Weight**: mass × gravity. A 10 000 kg rocket on Earth weighs about 98 kN.

**Wet mass**: the rocket's mass with propellant loaded.
