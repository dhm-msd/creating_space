# Venus

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gravity** | 8.87 m/s² (about 90 % of Earth's) |
| **Arrival height** | Y 300 |
| **Air** | None, **and it is hot**. See below. |
| **Cost from Earth Orbit** | 2 200 m/s (direct) |
| **Cost from the Earth's surface** | 3 700 m/s |
| **Cost back to Earth Orbit / Moon Orbit / the Earth** | 1 800 / 2 000 / 2 000 m/s |

Venus is the most expensive and the most hostile destination: a dark blackstone landscape under an orange sky, with ash drifting in the air and lava everywhere.

## Terrain and biomes

- The default rock is **blackstone**, and the default fluid is **lava**. The world's "sea level" is Y 55, so low areas are lava lakes.
- **Venus** is the main biome; **Venus Hellground** is a second variant. Both shed falling ash.
- Lava springs, fire patches and magma blocks appear, and canyons cut the terrain.
- The ground uses Minecraft's usual Overworld mix of surface blocks (stone, gravel, sand, terracotta, snow and more) over the blackstone.
- Venus has no orbit world of its own; you fly there straight from Earth Orbit or Moon Orbit.

## The heat

Venus adds a second hazard on top of having no air:

- Without a full spacesuit **and** an oxygen backtank that still has oxygen, you take **2 damage per second from suffocation plus 0.5 damage per second from overheating**.
- Wearing the full suit with a charged backtank protects you from both. It uses 1 unit of oxygen per second, exactly as elsewhere.
- Other creatures (everything except players and skeletons) take both kinds of damage in **any** airless place, Venus or not, unless they are inside a pressurised room; see [Life Support](Life-Support.md#who-is-affected).

## Fire

On Venus, placing a flammable block (wood, planks, leaves, wool, and so on) while you are **not** standing in a pressurised room sets fire to the space above it, or next to it if the space above is taken. Placing a **sapling** turns it into fire instead. Placing grass, dirt, podzol, mycelium, rooted dirt or coarse dirt turns the block you placed into **soul sand**.

Inside a sealed, pressurised room these rules do not apply, so build farms and gardens indoors. See [Oxygen Sealer and Pressurised Rooms](Oxygen-Sealer.md).

## Resources

Venus has no mod-specific ores. Expect Minecraft's own materials and magma.

## Structures

None.

## Surviving

- Rockets landing here fall at about 12.2 blocks per second.
- Fall damage is nearly the same as at home (8.87 ÷ 9.81 = 90 %).
