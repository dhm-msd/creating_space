# Creating Space Wiki

**Creating Space** is an add-on for [Create](https://create.fandom.com/wiki/Create_Wiki) that lets you design your own rocket engines, build rockets as Create contraptions, and fly them to other worlds. The goal is a science-flavoured space-race: real rocket-equation maths under the hood, but everything is automatable and playable with Create-style machines.

> [!NOTE]
> This wiki documents **Creating Space 1.7.23 for Minecraft 1.21.1 (NeoForge)**. Every number and rule in it was read from the mod's source code and data files rather than guessed. See [the folder README](README.md) for how it was verified and what to do if something looks out of date.

## What you can do in the mod

| Activity | Short version | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Design rocket engines | Pick a power pack, nozzle, propellant, size, thrust and expansion ratio at the *Rocket Engineer Table*. The mod computes pressure, temperature, ISP and mass for you, and tells you which metal can survive it. | [Rocket Engines](Rocket-Engines.md) |
| Build and launch rockets | A rocket is a Create contraption started from a *Rocket Controls* block. Fuel is stored in ordinary Create fluid tanks. | [Building a Rocket](Building-a-Rocket.md), [Launching and Flight](Launching-and-Flight.md) |
| Travel between worlds | Earth, Earth orbit, the Moon (and its orbit), Mars (and its orbit) and Venus. Each trip has a fixed Δv cost. | [Planets and Travel](Planets-and-Travel.md) |
| Survive in space | Vacuum hurts. Wear a full spacesuit with an oxygen backtank, or pressurise a sealed room with an *Oxygen Sealer*. | [Life Support](Life-Support.md) |
| Produce propellants | Liquefy air, split water, synthesise methane. | [Fluids and Propellants](Fluids-and-Propellants.md), [Machines](Machines.md) |
| Mine new metals | Nickel, aluminium and cobalt, plus several engine alloys. | [Resources and Materials](Resources-and-Materials.md) |
