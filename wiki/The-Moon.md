# The Moon

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gravity** | 1.6 m/s² (about one sixth of Earth's) |
| **Arrival height** | Y 300 |
| **Air** | None. You need a [spacesuit](Spacesuit-and-Oxygen-Backtank.md) or a [sealed room](Oxygen-Sealer.md). |

The Moon is the first off-world destination: cratered grey plains, lunar caves, rich metal ores, and a handful of wrecks that hold the blueprints for better engines.

## Terrain and biomes

- **Moon Plains** is the surface biome. The ground is **Moon Surface Regolith** over **Moon Regolith** over **Moon Stone**.
- **Moon Cave** is the underground biome (dripstone-cave music, and many more skeletons spawn here).
- **Craters** are carved out by the world generator: wide bowls with a radius of roughly 8 to 27 blocks.
- Caves also exist underground.

Both biomes are airless. The sky has the Moon's own look.

## Resources

| Resource | Notes |
|---|---|
| Moon Stone | The base rock. Needs a pickaxe. Crush it into Moon Regolith, wash the regolith for chances at gold, nickel, aluminium and cobalt nuggets. Stonecut it into bricks or polished stone. |
| Moon Nickel Ore | Common (20 veins per chunk). Iron pickaxe. |
| Moon Aluminum Ore | 10 veins per chunk. Iron pickaxe. |
| Moon Cobalt Ore | 5 veins per chunk. Diamond pickaxe. |

Full details on [Resources and Materials](Resources-and-Materials.md).

## Structures

All in Moon Plains: the **Crashed Rocket**, **Crashed Ship** and the rare **Abandoned Outpost**. The Crashed Rocket holds a **Design Blueprint**. See [Structures and Loot](Structures-and-Loot.md).

## Creatures

**Skeletons** are the only creature that spawns. They do not suffocate in vacuum (the mod exempts them), so they will be waiting for you. Spawn weight is 1 on the surface biome and 100 in the caves.

## Surviving

- The airless rules are on [Life Support](Life-Support.md): without a full suit you take 2 health points of damage per second.
- Fall damage is scaled down: you fall slowly and a given drop hurts a sixth as much as it would at home.
- Rockets landing here fall at about 7.8 blocks per second.
