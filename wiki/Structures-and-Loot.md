# Structures and Loot

Creating Space adds four generated structures, all in space. There are none in the Overworld, Earth Orbit, Moon Orbit, Mars Orbit or Venus.

| Structure | Where | Frequency* | Loot |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Crashed Rocket** | [The Moon](The-Moon.md), Moon Plains biome | one attempt per 20×20 chunks, at least 6 chunks apart | One barrel: **one Design Blueprint** (random among Ox Rich Flow, Fuel Rich Flow, Aerospike, Full Flow) plus **2–6 nickel ingots** |
| **Crashed Ship** | The Moon, Moon Plains biome | one attempt per 10×10 chunks, at least 6 chunks apart | None found in the data |
| **Abandoned Outpost** | The Moon, Moon Plains biome | one attempt per 80×80 chunks, at least 16 chunks apart (rare) | See note |
| **Underground Outpost** | [Mars](Mars.md), Mars Plains biome | one attempt per 10×10 chunks, at least 6 chunks apart | Seven barrels, each with the **Catalyse Cycle** Design Blueprint plus **2–6 cobalt ingots** |

\* The numbers are Minecraft's `random_spread` placement values (spacing and separation, in chunks). Higher spacing means rarer.

> [!NOTE]
> The data contains a loot table for the Abandoned Outpost (the same four designs as the Crashed Rocket, plus 1–3 cobalt ingots), but the outpost's structure files do not reference a loot container that uses it. Do not rely on the outpost as a guaranteed source of design blueprints.

## Tips

- The Moon's four-design pool means you may need to explore several Crashed Rockets to collect all four designs. Because each barrel gives only one, trading blueprints with other players (they are not consumed on use) is worthwhile.
- Moon structures are in the vacuum. Bring a [spacesuit](Spacesuit-and-Oxygen-Backtank.md).
- The Mars outpost is placed underground: the structure starts about 30 blocks below the surface (its start height is −30 relative to the terrain). Look for signs of it while digging in Mars Plains.
