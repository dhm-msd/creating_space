# Mars

| | |
|---|---|
| **Gravity** | 3.71 m/s² (a little over a third of Earth's) |
| **Arrival height** | Y 300 |
| **Air** | None. You need a [spacesuit](Spacesuit-and-Oxygen-Backtank.md) or a [sealed room](Oxygen-Sealer.md). |
| **Cost from Earth Orbit** | 1 300 m/s (via Mars Orbit: 1 000 + 300) |
| **Cost from the Earth's surface** | 2 800 m/s |
| **Cost back to Mars Orbit / Earth Orbit / the Earth** | 400 / 900 / 1 100 m/s |

Mars is a red, dry, airless desert with underground crystal geodes and a buried research outpost.

## Terrain and biomes

- **Mars Plains** is the surface biome. The ground is **Mars Surface Regolith** over **Mars Regolith** over **Mars Stone**. The world has no oceans or lakes.
- **Mars Cave** is the underground biome, where the nickel sulfate geodes generate.
- Cave carvers cut tunnels through the stone.

Both biomes are airless. The Mars biomes define no mob spawns.

## Resources

| Resource | Notes |
|---|---|
| Mars Stone | The base rock. Crushing it gives **Mars Regolith**. |
| Mars Regolith | Washing it gives chances at **cobalt** (12.5 % for 3 nuggets), **nickel** (8 % for 3) and **aluminium** (4 % for 3). This is a way to get cobalt without a diamond pickaxe. |
| **Nickel Sulfate geodes** | A shell of Crimsite and Veridium around crystals. Break the clusters for **Nickel Sulfate Shards**, the key ingredient of the **Nickel Sulfate Catalyst**. See [Resources and Materials](Resources-and-Materials.md#nickel-sulfate-geodes-mars). |

## Structures

The **Underground Outpost**: a buried base in Mars Plains with seven barrels, each holding the **Catalyse Cycle** design blueprint and 2 to 6 cobalt ingots. See [Structures and Loot](Structures-and-Loot.md).

## Surviving

- Without a full suit and a charged oxygen backtank you take 2 health points of damage per second. See [Life Support](Life-Support.md).
- Rockets landing here fall at about 9.2 blocks per second.
- Fall damage is scaled down to 38 % (3.71 ÷ 9.81).
